Account & Data Deletion

ZIM POS · Developer: Zimrion · Android package com.zimrion.restopos · Last updated: 20 October 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your ZIM POS account and the data associated with it, in line with Google Play's data-deletion requirements.

How to request deletion

Because a ZIM POS business account (restaurant or retail shop) is created and managed by us for each business, deletion is handled on request. To delete your account and data, contact us using any of the following and include your business name, your Account ID and the registered phone number so we can verify you own the account:

What gets deleted

  • Your business account (restaurant or retail shop) and license record.
  • Staff records (names, phones, roles and PIN hashes).
  • Business data: orders, bills, Custom Bills, expenses, inventory and stock, suppliers, recipes, sessions and reports.
  • Any customer names/phone numbers stored on orders or Custom Bills.
  • Product and menu images and the business logo.
  • Cloud backups of the above stored on our backup provider.

You can also remove the locally stored data yourself at any time by logging out and uninstalling the App from your device(s).

Timeline

We will verify and process deletion requests within 30 days. Some records may be retained for a short additional period only where required to meet legal, tax or fraud-prevention obligations, after which they are deleted.
