This Privacy Policy explains how Zimrion (“we”, “us”) accesses, collects, uses, shares, protects, retains and deletes information when you use ZIM POS (“the App”), a point-of-sale and business-management app for restaurants and retail shops. It applies to the ZIM POS Android app and the ZIM POS service it connects to.

1. Who uses the App

ZIM POS is a business tool. It is used by business owners and their staff (cashiers). Each business receives an Account ID and a licence key from us, and its staff sign in with a personal 6-digit PIN. The business is responsible for the information its staff enter about the business and its customers. The App is not intended for children.

2. Information we collect and process

  • Business account information — business name, owner name, city, address, contact phone number, the Account ID and licence key, subscription status, and business type (restaurant or retail).
  • Staff information — staff name, optional phone number, role (Owner or Cashier) and a 6-digit PIN. The PIN is never stored in plain text; only a salted one-way hash is kept.
  • Business records — products and menu items (names, prices, categories, optional cost prices and barcodes), sales and order records (items, totals, payment method, bill numbers), shifts, expenses, stock and inventory records, recipes and ingredients, suppliers, Custom Bills, reports, and receipt settings (such as the name, phone, address and tax rate printed on bills).
  • Customer details (optional) — a customer name and phone number, only if staff choose to enter them on an order or Custom Bill. A supplier name and phone number may also be entered.
  • Images — product photos and a business logo, only when the user chooses to add them from the device camera or photo gallery.
  • Device and technical information — a randomly generated device identifier, a device label (for example “Android · 1A2B”) and the platform name, used to manage which devices are signed in to a business; and the IP address and request details that our server records in its logs when the App connects.

We do not collect precise or approximate location, contacts, SMS, call logs, browsing history or advertising identifiers. The App contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. ZIM POS records which payment method a sale used (for example Cash, Card, JazzCash or EasyPaisa); it does not process payments or collect card numbers.

3. How the information is used

  • To provide the App’s features: billing and point of sale, order history, products, stock and inventory, expenses, reports and profit & loss, Custom Bills and receipt printing.
  • To verify the licence and subscription, sign staff in, and apply Owner and Cashier permissions.
  • To keep a business’s data available across its signed-in devices, and to create backups so data can be restored.
  • To keep the service secure and working: detecting misuse, preventing unauthorised access, and diagnosing errors.
  • To provide support when you contact us.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising.

4. Where the information is stored

  • On our server — business data is stored on our server, hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS), so it is available to the business’s devices and for reports.
  • On your device — the App keeps a cached copy of the product list, app settings, and any sales recorded while offline until they are sent to the server. The licence key and the staff sign-in session are kept in the device’s encrypted secure storage.
  • Backups — compressed backup copies of business data are stored with Cloudflare R2, which encrypts stored data at rest.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. We use the following service providers, which process data on our behalf only to run the App:

  • Amazon Web Services — server hosting.
  • Cloudflare — storage of backups (Cloudflare R2).
  • Expo (650 Industries) — delivery of app updates; when the App checks for an update it sends technical information such as the app version, platform and an installation identifier.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users or others.

Some actions send information outside the App only when you choose them: sharing a bill or report as a PDF through your device’s share menu, or opening WhatsApp or the phone dialer to contact support. Receipts printed on Bluetooth or USB printers are sent directly from your device to the printer.

6. Device permissions

  • Camera — to scan product barcodes and to take a product or logo photo when you choose to. Barcode scanning is processed on the device; camera images are not uploaded unless you save a photo to a product or as your logo.
  • Photos — to pick a product photo or logo from your gallery when you choose to.
  • Bluetooth — to find and connect to Bluetooth receipt printers. ZIM POS does not use Bluetooth to determine your location.
  • Internet — to connect to the ZIM POS server.

7. Security

  • Current supported releases of the ZIM POS Android app (version 1.2.0 and later) communicate with our server only over encrypted HTTPS connections, and the app does not allow unencrypted network traffic. Older versions should be updated from Google Play.
  • Staff PINs are stored only as salted one-way hashes.
  • Access to a business’s data requires its Account ID, licence key and a staff PIN. Owner-only areas, such as reports, profit & loss, expenses, staff, devices and settings, are restricted to the Owner, and these permissions are enforced on our server.
  • Secrets such as licence keys, PINs and access tokens are removed from our server logs.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Data retention

  • Business data is kept while the business’s account is active, so the App and its reports keep working.
  • Backups are rotated automatically; the oldest backup copies are kept for up to about 12 months.
  • Server logs are rotated automatically and kept only for a limited period.
  • Data stored on a device stays there until you sign out or uninstall the App.

Deactivating or locking an account stops access to it but does not delete its data. Data is deleted when you ask us to delete it (see below).

9. Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your business account and its data at any time. Contact us at imranrazzaq971@gmail.com or on WhatsApp/phone +92 317 0131160 from the business’s registered contact, with the business name and Account ID. We verify the request and then delete the account, staff records, business records, customer details, images and our backup copies. We complete deletion within 30 days; some records may be kept for a short additional period only where required by law. Full details are on our Account & Data Deletion page. An Owner can also remove a staff member in the App (Settings → Staff); this stops that person signing in, but their staff record is kept with the business’s records until the business’s data is deleted. Anyone can remove the data stored on a device by signing out and uninstalling the App.
